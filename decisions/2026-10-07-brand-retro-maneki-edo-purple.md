# Brand identity: retro Japanese print maneki-neko, Edo purple, custom LUCKY lettering

- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Deciders:** Lucas Santana
- **Scope:** Marketing and identity assets (`assets/`, `packages/frontend/public/og-image.png`,
  `packages/frontend/public/{lucky-logo,favicon}.{png,webp}`, `packages/frontend/branding/`).
  The dashboard UI colour tokens are out of scope.
- **Supersedes:** `decisions/2026-10-07-brand-purple-gold-wordmark-family.md` (purple and gold
  neon, the original neon cat, three wordmarks by use).

## Context

The purple and gold neon system shipped in #2689 the same day. Looking at it in place, the
owner said it "still looks very generic AI": the neon glow, the cat drawing, the composition
and the colours. Agent-drawn SVG cats had already failed four times, so the next round was
anchored on real, human-made references instead of more process.

A board of 12 maneki-neko pieces by working designers (Dribbble) was built; the owner picked
three: a flat retro maneki-neko on a red sun (Rick Calzi), a retro print with large 招き猫
kanji (Zellene Guanlao), and a mascot plus wordmark rebrand (Jesslin Lee). What they share,
and the neon never had: flat spot colour, two or three inks, no glow, a deliberate line, and a
real print heritage. The references were described to the image model in words only; none of
them was fed to it or traced.

## Decision

- **The cat** is a traditional ceramic maneki-neko (big round head, calico patches, red collar
  with a bell, a stamped koban without characters) with a **wink**, drawn as flat retro print
  with one thick ink outline. Source: `packages/frontend/branding/source/cat-wink-src.png`,
  generated in Gemini (Nano Banana Pro) through rounds the owner steered. It is never redrawn:
  `cat_cutout.py` cuts it by flood fill from the cream background. The model drew the open eye
  as a human almond; the owner found it "olho muito humano" and picked a solid ink oval (like a
  vintage toy) over a gold slit pupil, stamped by `open_eye()` in the same script.
- **Signature colour: Edo purple (江戸紫) `#52387B`.** Maneki-neko branding is almost always red
  and gold; purple is ownable in the category, comes from the same Edo era as the koban, and
  keeps continuity with Lucky's earlier purple. Supporting inks: Ink `#211D2E`, Vermilion
  `#B83A24` (collar, small accents), Gold `#C9922E` (koban), Cream paper `#F3E6CB`.
- **Primary lockup (version A):** ink square frame, Edo purple sun disc, the cat seated on the
  frame's bottom line, LUCKY below. The owner chose A over the inverted B (purple field, cream
  disc), which is kept for the Discord avatar and dark surfaces.
- **Wordmark:** the chunky rounded LUCKY lettering from the approved A artwork, vectorised
  with potrace into `branding/source/wordmark.svg`. It is custom lettering, not a font.
  Supporting type: Dela Gothic One (OFL; eyebrows, katakana ラッキー) and Manrope 600 (body).
- **Small sizes:** the app icon is the cat's head in a purple disc (`public/favicon.png`,
  `public/lucky-logo.png`), legible at 32 px.
- **No neon, glow, gradients, sparkles or 3D** anywhere in brand assets, including generated
  product surfaces: the weekly recap card (`packages/render`, shipped in v2.53.0 with the neon
  wordmark and glow) is redrawn in this language before its first Sunday post (owner,
  2026-10-07).

## Consequences

- Banners, og-image, avatar and app icon are regenerated by `cat_cutout.py` and `banners.py`;
  the neon cat master (`cat-neon.png`) is removed.
- The seigaiha pattern explored in #2695 (purple neon lines) does not fit the new system and
  is dropped.
- The dashboard sidebar, login and landing show the new round icon through `lucky-logo.png`;
  the UI accent stays pink until the owner decides otherwise.
- The cat source is a ~1000 px screen capture of the model output. A full-resolution download
  (owner action) should replace `cat-wink-src.png` before any print use.
- Still open: the owner uploads `branding/source/avatar-1024.png` and `assets/lucky-bot-banner.png`
  in the Discord Developer Portal.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the neon system and refine it:** rejected by the owner as generic AI.
- **Asagi teal or vermilion as the signature colour:** teal has no tie to Lucky; vermilion is
  the category default.
- **A stock rounded font for the wordmark (Mochiy Pop One, Rammetto One, Titan One):** close,
  but the approved lettering is already custom and ownable.
